## Feedback lifetime and capacity

`notificationState.ts` owns admission, deadlines, repeat grouping, retention and display selection. `NotificationProvider` stays above responsive workspaces and schedules one timer for the earliest active deadline. A toast's five seconds start when the provider accepts it. Adding a neighboring notice, changing preferences, resizing or opening Recent feedback preserves that deadline. Time spent in a hidden tab counts toward expiry; returning to the tab settles overdue notices.

The overlay loads when the first notice arrives, keeping its view code outside mobile startup. Admission and timing remain in the mounted provider. While the view chunk loads, or if it is unavailable, a plain, bounded message list retains feedback and dismissal while the workspace stays mounted. Ordinary notices remain visible on their original deadlines in this fallback. The overlay shows up to three toasts and one banner. **Recent feedback** makes other retained records available in a scrollable panel, including on mobile. Ordinary information expires completely. Warnings, errors, critical notices, error-linked information and persistent feedback remain reviewable after their transient presentation ends. Banners and explicitly persistent notices receive no expiry timer. Each message has a named Dismiss control; Clear feedback clears the list and capacity receipt while preserving preferences and accepted work.

The list retains up to 50 detailed records. At capacity, ordinary information yields space first; an incoming optional notice cannot replace a retained attention-bearing record. When every retained record requires attention, a new attention-bearing notice replaces the oldest record. The panel discloses this policy and reports omitted details. This rotation removes the old message and correlation reference; the synthetic Error Lab log is a separate example and cannot recover it. Persistent means timer-independent: dismissal, Clear, page reload and this disclosed capacity rotation end retention. The list is a bounded interaction aid, rather than a durable operational audit record.

An exact repeat within its active delivery window increments a visible occurrence count, returns to the latest visible slot and keeps the original deadline. Capacity rotation follows accepted time, independently of this presentation ordering. Severity, kind, persistence and correlation reference all participate in that comparison. A repeat after expiry creates fresh visible feedback. Grouping affects presentation only; accepted domain operations retain their own identity. Occurrence and omission counters saturate at `9999+`. The named limits live with the reducer and can be revised with its receiving checks.

A personal display preference remains independent of record-write permissions. Reload starts with informational toasts enabled and an empty feedback list; the existing theme owner separately remembers appearance.

The Preferences panel reuses the existing compact switch and theme treatments. Unsupported density, animation, reflection, automatic-comparison and default-view settings have retired because they had no consuming behavior. Notifications states the availability of Slack, email and mobile-push delivery directly. Connections and Error Lab retain their explicit previews; they neither contact external services nor change operational records.

Focused reducer checks cover required feedback, current/future optional filtering, reset, stable deadlines, repeats, hidden expiry and retention priority under a burst. Controlled-clock browser journeys distinguish a neighboring notice at four seconds from the original five-second expiry, including dismissal and preference changes; mobile checks retain persistent feedback and verify bounded history scrolling. Browser journeys at 1024 and 1512 pixels verify separated actor/inspection context, a fully reachable Access Grid and selected-section retention through desktop resizing. Preference journeys observe the real notification overlay, shared appearance, navigation/viewport retention, reset and permission refusals. The startup bundle check protects the mobile budget. Rendered-pair checks cover actual notification titles, messages and the recent-feedback panel in Light and High contrast.

This contract supersedes the earlier disabled preference/routing controls. External notification delivery remains a separate future integration.
