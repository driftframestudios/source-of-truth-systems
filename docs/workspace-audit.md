## State and action contract

Workflow resources live in the shared browser-session owner and initialize when received. Navigation and responsive layouts preserve accepted edits. Reload begins a fresh session for local workflows; an installed, unexpired [Source workspace](visitor-workspaces.md) rehydrates its saved metadata. Drafts remain with their editors, with validation and refusal leaving them available for correction where specified. Theme preference uses its separate browser-local persistence. Access Grid’s **Reset scenario grants** restores all example grants and clears that grid’s draft while retaining the acting and inspected selections, accepted work and exploration. It is recovery of the public experiment, including in read-only scenarios; saving grants retains its authority checks. Compact desktop composition changes through CSS and preserves mounted editor content; the [layout contract](workspace-layout.md) identifies the task-specific rail, disclosure and return owners.

Each command resolves the exact owned record, rechecks current authority and validates its supported transition or fields. Versioned editors also check the revision they opened. Recovery is domain-specific: resume/reopen, restore bundled metadata, restore an original release record, discard a proposal, or reopen/save a separate cohort definition. A successful permission preview alone never reports completed work.

Measured QC outcomes, recorded artifact acceptance and current review responses remain distinct. Source authority describes provenance; Access Grid grants describe user access. Map system examples and public release/source/artifact records use capability checks appropriate to their own records, without fabricating patient geography. Canonical specimen and Plate commands retain their consent/resource scope.

Profile's optional informational-toast preference affects actual local notices. Required feedback, errors, warnings and banners remain available. Unsupported preference controls have retired; intentional diagnostic previews identify their actual effect. The application keeps external refresh, file generation, release publication and remote delivery behind their explicit future execution boundaries.

## Language contract

- Operational guidance names the responsible actor or system, the work and its consequence. Current behavior leads; a future evaluation has its own receiving decision.
- Consent, privacy, legal and access restrictions retain their exact scope. Research explains its de-identified browser-session examples and states, “Re-identification is prohibited.” A recovery step offers permitted action rather than additional authority.
- Local acceptance names the actual result and lifetime. A review response, metadata proposal or rule definition does not claim laboratory execution, a generated file or a patient export.
- Historical wording, clinical facts, quotations and stable identifiers retain source fidelity. Any new clinical/legal conclusion requires its own evidence review.
- Empty or denied results explain the available recovery. Boolean NOT and executable assertions retain their logical meaning.

This register improves human inspectability. Future embedded AI needs its own grounded behavior evaluation before these records become an instruction or answer source.
