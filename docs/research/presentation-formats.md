# Presentation formats: evidence and decision

Visitors can review the same source-owned brief at three reading speeds: a message-led Briefing, a chapter-based Evidence review, and a continuous Report. The [presentation contract](../presentation-styles.md) describes the resulting implementation.

## Evidence that changed the design

| Source and evidence class | Relevant finding or guidance | Receiving design decision and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Garner, Alley, Wolfe and Sawarynski, 2013; author research summary](https://writing.engr.psu.edu/research.html), controlled engineering-education study | Audiences heard the same explanation while viewing different slide structures. Message headlines supported by evidence improved measured understanding and retention relative to topic-and-bullet slides. | Briefing pairs one source headline with its supporting measures, sequence, comparison or explanation. This is a design inference for a showcase, not a measured comprehension gain in this application. |
| [Harvard Catalyst slide guidance](https://catalyst.harvard.edu/writing-communication-center/visualize-science/slides/), institutional communication practice | Strong headlines, relevant visual evidence, clear labels and restrained animation support the audience's reading task. | Units, qualifier notes and labels stay beside values; navigation reveals an entire source section. Existing material supplies the evidence; generated scientific figures are outside this change. |
| [Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style*, §§1.1–1.2](https://s3.amazonaws.com/arena-attachments/2584673/e36028a1643a616ec4be1e8d0e859fbb.pdf), typographic craft reference | Typography should express the text's structure; differentiated heading levels, related captions and incidental identity establish a coherent reading order. | Report uses quieter headings, adjacent explanatory content, a running deck identity and an approximately 78ch maximum reading column. That responsive measure is a local convention to verify in the browser, not a universal rule or a claim that the excerpt specifies screen dimensions. |
| [UNC Writing Center: Scientific Reports](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/scientific-reports/), academic writing guidance | Scientific report organization helps readers distinguish the work's purpose, methods, results and interpretation; conventions depend on the discipline. | Report keeps the sections actually present in each example. It does not fabricate missing methods, outcomes or IMRAD sections. |
| [Jambor et al., 2021](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3001161), meta-research on image-based figures in 580 papers | Common interpretability gaps included missing scale information and incomplete explanations of colors, labels and depicted objects. | Every format carries source labels, units and qualifiers with its evidence. The paper studies published scientific images; this application-specific transfer remains a design inference. |

## Options and synthesis

A single recolored player preserved the old behavior but offered one reading pace. Separate independent templates could vary format substantially while creating several competing copies of the same content. An automatic summary mode would require an additional factuality and editing contract. A shared section model with explicit format selection gives visitors structural variation and keeps one source sequence.

The selected design retains the six existing visual identities as a separate choice from format. Briefing presents one section at a time; Evidence review keeps the expressive deck compositions with chapter navigation; Report gives the same material a continuous reading column and contents. Changing format preserves the current source section. Artifact composition uses the same path and supplies recorded artifact facts and acceptance checks.

## Evidence and future boundary

The unit observation checks source identity, section labels and the three navigation contracts. Production-browser journeys check two distinct deck identities, section continuity, the final reachable content, shared style overrides, artifact picks, desktop and compact laptop layouts, reduced motion and High contrast paint. These observations establish usable rendering and state transitions on the exercised paths; evaluating audience understanding would require a separate study.

The typed identity includes a stable source kind, and sections retain their source content object. A later generated-outline adapter can target that boundary when its own source/claim policy is approved. This delivery performs local projection of existing examples and records, with no AI calls. The [visual contract](../presentation-styles.md) owns current composition and interaction details.
