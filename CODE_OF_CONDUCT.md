# Community conduct

Make repository discussions useful and welcoming. Treat people with respect, explain technical disagreements through evidence and consequences, and give others room to ask questions or revise a proposal. Critique the work without attacking the person.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats and publishing another person's private information are outside this community's boundaries. Keep shared examples synthetic and discuss disagreements in the relevant issue or PR. The maintainer may clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful material, close a discussion or restrict participation to keep the repository workable.

Vikrant Varun Kudesia is the repository maintainer. For a conduct concern, use an existing private contact route only when the maintainer has provided one. Otherwise, ask for a private conversation through a repository issue without including sensitive details or repeating harmful content. Share the details after a private route is agreed. This policy does not imply a staffed reporting service or response-time promise.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests and project discussions. [Support](SUPPORT.md) and [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) describe the ordinary working routes.
